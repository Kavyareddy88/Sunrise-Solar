SMARTER SOLAR LIVING

Make the most of every sunny day.

Small everyday choices can help you get more from your solar system. Explore a few simple ways to make clean energy part of your routine.

01
USE DAYLIGHT

Make the most of the sun.

When practical, run energy-intensive appliances during daylight hours while your solar system is generating electricity.

02
TRACK PERFORMANCE

Stay connected to your energy.

Check your inverter or monitoring app regularly to understand your system's generation patterns and notice unusual changes.

03
PANEL CARE

Give your panels the right care.

Follow your installer's maintenance guidance and keep panels clear of excessive dirt and avoidable shading.
