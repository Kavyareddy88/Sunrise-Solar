HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions.

A few helpful answers before you take your next step towards solar.

How accurate is this solar savings calculator?

It provides an illustrative estimate based on the monthly bill and selected bill offset. A detailed assessment is needed to estimate your actual savings.

Why do I need to enter my monthly electricity bill?

Your monthly bill provides a starting point for estimating your current electricity expenses and exploring potential savings.

Will my actual savings match the estimate?

Not necessarily. Actual savings depend on your property's solar generation, electricity usage, tariff, system design and applicable utility rules.

Do I need a rooftop assessment?

Yes. A rooftop assessment helps review available space, shading, orientation and other practical factors before a system is recommended.

How can I get a personalised solar estimate?

Contact our team to discuss your electricity usage and arrange a site assessment for your property.
