NEED A LITTLE CLARITY?

Frequently Asked Questions.

Find answers to common questions about solar services, installation and support.

What solar service is right for my property?

The right solution depends on your energy usage, property type, available roof space and goals. A site assessment can help identify suitable options.

Do you provide residential and commercial solutions?

Yes. Sunrise Solar offers solutions for residential properties and commercial facilities, subject to project requirements.

How do I arrange a site survey?

Contact our team through the consultation form to discuss your property and arrange the next steps.

Can I add battery storage to my solar system?

Battery compatibility depends on the inverter, system configuration and your energy requirements. We can help you review suitable options.

Does a solar system require maintenance?

Periodic inspection and appropriate cleaning help keep a solar installation in good operating condition.
