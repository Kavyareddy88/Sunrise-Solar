OUR STORY

Making clean energy feel closer to home.

We started with a simple belief: choosing solar energy should feel clear, practical and rewarding. Every household deserves the opportunity to explore cleaner ways of powering its home.

Sunrise Solar brings together guidance, planning and solar solutions to make that journey easier. We take the time to understand each home, explain the available options and help customers make informed decisions.

Our focus goes beyond installation. We believe in building lasting relationships through dependable support, honest communication and thoughtful service.
